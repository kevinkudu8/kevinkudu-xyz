Arc Studio was the first event series for Arc, Circle's new stablecoin-native blockchain, and the launch of its Architect community. We created and produced it from the ground up, timed to kick off Devconnect in Buenos Aires.

It was small by design: 585 people registered for a room built for 250, and we curated it down to the builders, founders and investors who matter most to Arc's early ecosystem, with developers from 26 countries. The format worked well enough to become a series, with a second edition at ETHDenver.
