Arc Studio was the first event series for Arc, Circle's new stablecoin-native blockchain, and the launch of its Architect community. We created and produced it from the ground up, timed to kick off Devconnect in Buenos Aires.

It was small by design. We curated the room from hundreds of applicants to bring together the builders, founders and investors who matter most to Arc's early ecosystem, and it filled to capacity with developers from 26 countries. The format worked well enough to become a series, with a second edition at ETHDenver.
