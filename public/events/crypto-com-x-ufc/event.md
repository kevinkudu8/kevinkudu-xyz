For UFC International Fight Week, Crypto.com wanted to own the moment in Las Vegas. The campaign ran on four levels, anchored by a three-day pop-up at The Forum Shops at Caesars Palace that turned a credit card into something fans could step inside, play with and sign up for on the spot.

Around it: out-of-home across the Strip, a club night for influencers and the community, and a VIP track for thought leaders. The work came out of a PayPal × Crypto.com activation I'd managed before, and I supported execution alongside our events director.
