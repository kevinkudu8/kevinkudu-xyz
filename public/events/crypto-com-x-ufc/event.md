For UFC International Fight Week in Las Vegas, I helped run a large-scale experiential campaign for Crypto.com alongside my events director. The centrepiece was a 30×40 ft pop-up at The Forum Shops at Caesars Palace, built around the Crypto.com Visa Signature card to turn fight fans into sign-ups.

An out-of-home campaign along the Strip, including a 3D billboard near T-Mobile Arena, and influencer collaborations carried it well beyond the room. The project came out of ongoing outreach after a PayPal × Crypto.com activation I'd managed before.
