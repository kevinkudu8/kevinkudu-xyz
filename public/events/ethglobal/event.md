For a year and four months I was an event producer at ETHGlobal, running AV and video for the world's largest series of Ethereum hackathons: 35 events across 12 cities and online, from 2022 to 2023.

On site, that meant the stages, screens and cameras, and the crews behind them, often found and hired locally in each new city. Online, it meant the broadcast look of every virtual hackathon and summit, including a rebrand of ETHGlobal's video identity. Along the way I edited 600+ videos.
